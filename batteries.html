Where to Drop Off Batteries

Accepts Rechargeable batteries including:
• Nickel-Metal Hydride (NiMH)
• Nickel-Cadmium (NiCd)
• Lithium-ion (Li-ion)

Program limit: 11 lbs or less and 300 watt-hours or less per battery. Free drop-off for eligible batteries.

Home Depot partners with Call2Recycle for rechargeable battery collection. Call (773) 486-9200 to confirm the North Avenue store's collection bin is available and accepts your battery type. Store hours and collection availability may change.

Battery collection bins are typically near the entrance or Customer Service Desk. These are store hours, not separately confirmed drop-off hours. Call before visiting.
