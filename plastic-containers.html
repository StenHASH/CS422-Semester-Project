When You're On The Go

No sink nearby? You can still make a responsible disposal decision in a few quick steps.

1

Empty the container

Put leftover food and liquids into the trash. Remove utensils and other loose objects.

2

Wipe away residue

Use a napkin to scrape or wipe away sauce and food. A spotless container isn't required, but it must be empty and reasonably clean.

3

Make the bin decision

If food or liquid still remains and you can't clean it, use the trash. If it is empty and clean enough, check the plastic type and local recycling rules before using a recycling bin.

Important: Not every recycling bin accepts every plastic container. Check the bin label and your building or city's rules. Do not place food-filled containers in recycling.

Source: Recycle by City - Cleaning Recyclables
